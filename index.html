What I bring to an NVH team

Hands-on testing. I plan instrumentation, install accelerometers, microphones, strain gauges, and shakers myself, and run multi-channel tests in the lab and on site with Siemens LMS Testlab, eDAQ, and cDAQ.

Test-to-CAE correlation. I correlate measured modes and responses with ANSYS and Abaqus models, and update the models until they predict what the hardware does.

Data analysis and automation. I read spectra, orders, and transfer functions, and use MATLAB and Python to automate the analysis and cluster or classify what the data shows.

Clear communication. I write concise reports and recommendations that design, reliability, and hardware teams can act on.
