Impedance measurements and structural health monitoring

Pole-based approach to interpret electromechanical impedance measurements in structural health monitoring

ResultPublished in the Journal of Vibration Engineering & Technologies (2026).

This project interprets electromechanical impedance measurements through the poles of the system, which describe its resonances and damping, for structural health monitoring.

It was carried out within the NSF-funded cyber-physical system integrity project.

What I did

  • Estimated, analyzed, and tracked system poles to explain how impedance measurements change.
  • Studied the effects of 4 external sources on the measurements.
  • Developed data-driven algorithms to compensate for temperature variation.

Tools and methods

  • System pole estimation and tracking
  • Electromechanical impedance measurements
