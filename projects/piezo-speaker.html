Vibration, NVH, and structural dynamics

Piezoelectric-based speaker using smart materials and vibroacoustic modeling

This project turns an acrylic plate into a vibroacoustic speaker driven by piezoelectric elements.

What I did

  • Assisted in designing and modeling the plate, with structural and acoustic optimization.
  • Instrumented the structure with 6 strategically placed sensors to maximize acoustic resonance, which improved the overall response.

Tools and methods

  • Piezoelectric transducers
