Vibration, NVH, and structural dynamics

Structural and NVH analysis of a race car chassis

ResultChassis stiffness and 6 mode shapes checked against target values.

This project studies the equivalent torsional and bending stiffness of a race car chassis, along with its structural and NVH behavior.

What I did

  • Built Abaqus 3-D models to determine torsional and bending stiffness, supporting lightweight design.
  • Ran static and modal analyses to find stress hotspots and the modes that matter for noise and vibration.
  • Compared chassis stiffness and 6 FEA mode shapes with target values to verify stiffness and NVH requirements.

Tools and methods

  • Abaqus
  • Static and modal analysis
