Vibration, NVH, and structural dynamics

Free vibration analysis of homogeneous rectangular Mindlin plates with general boundary conditions and various loading functions

ResultPublished at ASME IMECE 2017.

This undergraduate research project models the free vibration of rectangular Mindlin plates, a plate theory that includes shear deformation, under general elastic boundary conditions.

What I did

  • Developed models for free vibrations of isotropic homogeneous rectangular Mindlin plates with variable thicknesses.
  • Simulated the response of the constrained structure using the Rayleigh-Ritz method.

Tools and methods

  • Rayleigh-Ritz method
  • Maple and MATLAB

Publication

Sangle, Shesha & Kiran (2017), ASME IMECE, Tampa, FL. Formulation of Problem of Free Vibration Analysis of Isotropic Homogeneous Rectangular Mindlin Plates Having Variable Thickness with General Elastic Boundary Conditions. DOI
