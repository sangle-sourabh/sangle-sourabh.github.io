Impedance measurements and structural health monitoring

Unique part identification using electromechanical impedance measurements via projection methods

Result100% precision and a 98% balanced F1-score in identifying parts.

This project uses the electromechanical impedance of a part, a measurement of its dynamic response, as a unique identity that is hard to clone.

It is part of an NSF-funded project on cyber-physical system integrity and security, in collaboration with 4 universities.

What I did

  • Built a vibration-based identification system that estimates structural parameters under varying external sources, with data-driven compensation.
  • Used statistical modeling and projection methods on electromechanical impedance measurements.
  • Led this work within an NSF-funded project across 4 universities.

Tools and methods

  • Electromechanical impedance measurements
  • Projection methods and statistical modeling
  • MATLAB
